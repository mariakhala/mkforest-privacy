Privacy Policy for MKforest

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Who we are

MKforest ("the app") is published by MMM Developers ("we"). Contact: muneeb1@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, decorations, daily streaks, and settings such as sound, comfort options and difficulty) is stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Unity Ads, a service of Unity Technologies: a small banner on the map and on the win, lose and pause screens of a level (never while you play), a full-screen ad between some levels, and video ads that you choose to watch for a reward. The game is set up in Unity Ads as directed to children with a mixed audience, so the ads are contextual (based on the game, not on you) and are not personalized. To show and measure ads, Unity Ads may collect limited technical data such as the device model, operating system version and how you interact with ads, as allowed for child-directed apps. You can reset or limit the advertising identifier in your phone's settings. Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

No purchases

This version of the app sells nothing for real money. Coins, lives and boosters are earned by playing or by choosing to watch a video ad. If in-app purchases are added in a later version, this page will be updated first. Links that leave the app (for example to rate the game in Google Play, or to open this page) are behind a short question for grown-ups.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your own device (for example "your lives are full" or "daily treats are ready"). They are created by the app itself; no data is sent for them. You can turn them off in your phone's settings at any time.

No chance-based rewards

The app has no random loot boxes, wheels or lucky draws. Every reward shows its exact contents before you get it.

No age question, no game statistics

The app does not ask for your age and treats every player the same way, as if a child is playing. It sends no game statistics or analytics to us or to anyone else.

Children

The app is a puzzle game for all ages, including children under 13. We do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses or locations, and there is no chat or sharing with other players. The nickname you can type for the Parlor League stays on your phone and is never sent anywhere. Ads are non-personalized and come only from Unity Ads, an ad network approved by Google Play for apps that children use. If you are a parent and have a question or want data deleted, contact us and we will help you request deletion from Unity.

Your choices and requests

You can reset the advertising identifier in your phone settings, turn off notifications, and delete all game data by uninstalling. For any question or deletion request, email the contact above.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when the app's data practices change, for example if online features such as cloud saving are added.
